Support

Write to the developer at support@mutoscope.app.

Questions and problems

Email any question about Mutoscope, or a problem you've run into. Say what you were doing, what happened, and which version of Mutoscope you have (Settings → About → Version).

In test versions (TestFlight), with diagnostics allowed, Report a Problem in Settings → Advanced, or holding Play/Pause anywhere in the app, sends the developer your description, a picture of the screen and the diagnostics log, straight from the Apple TV.

Deleting reports you've uploaded

To have the problem reports and crash logs you've sent from a test version deleted, email support@mutoscope.app with the Session shown in Settings → Advanced. The privacy policy says what those uploads hold and where they're kept.

Reporting a review or comment

Hold Select on a Trakt or AniList review or comment to report it. When your profile has a linked Trakt account, a Trakt post is reported to Trakt's moderators; otherwise the report comes to the developer. Reported posts, and the posts of authors you block, are hidden on your profile; Settings → Account → Community can show them again.

You can also email support@mutoscope.app about a post: say which title it was on and who wrote it.

Source code of mpv and FFmpeg

Mutoscope plays video with mpv and FFmpeg, which are licensed under the GNU LGPL 2.1 or later. They and the libraries they use are built into Libmpv.framework, a separate dynamic library inside the app, so it can be replaced with another build of them. The libraries are used unmodified except for mpv, which has two changes: a video output that hands frames to tvOS, and a filesystem check left out on tvOS.

For the complete source code of these libraries as built, including those changes, write to support@mutoscope.app. The full list of libraries and their licences is in the app, in Settings → About → Acknowledgements.
